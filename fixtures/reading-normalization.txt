CIS 8040 — Database Normalization Reading
Chapter 3: Functional Dependencies and Normal Forms

Key idea: A relation is in Third Normal Form (3NF) when every non-prime attribute
is non-transitively dependent on every candidate key.

Armstrong's axioms:
1. Reflexivity
2. Augmentation  
3. Transitivity

Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF) is stricter than 3NF: for every non-trivial FD X -> A,
X must be a superkey.

Example: STUDENT_COURSE(student_id, course_id, instructor, office)
may violate BCNF if instructor -> office and instructor is not a key.
